01 · ABOUT

From Lomé to a low-level control loop

I'm an embedded software engineer completing an engineering double-degree (Computer Science — Embedded Systems & Robotics, plus a M.Sc. in Complex Systems / Mechatronics) at UTBM, France. I started my studies in Togo with a B.Sc. in Software Engineering before moving to France to specialize in embedded and real-time systems.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware and software: writing low-level C on ARM Cortex-M4 targets to close real-time control loops (power electronics, motor and current regulation), designing multi-task firmware architectures under FreeRTOS, and wiring the communication stacks — CAN, Modbus, MQTT, gRPC — that let embedded systems talk to the rest of the world.

I've built this across three placements: transforming a UPS into a high-performance test current source at SOCOMEC, developing the firmware of a connected air purifier at TECHNOPURE, and building microgrid control-command logic at EDF R&D. I'm now looking for a full-time role — ideally in energy or transportation (aerospace, rail, automotive) firmware — and I'm genuinely open to relocating internationally to find the right team.

03 · EXPERIENCE

Where I've shipped firmware

FEB 2026 — JUL 2026

Embedded Software Engineer — Power Electronics Internship

SOCOMEC SAS · Huttenheim, France

Transforming a UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) into a high-performance test current source.

  • Developed low-level control laws in C on an Infineon XMC4500 (ARM Cortex-M4) to drive power conversion.
  • Implemented a current regulation loop (ABC then Clarke/αβ, PI anti-windup) under strict real-time constraints using hardware Capture & Compare timers.
  • Validated control logic in simulation with Matlab Coder / Simulink before experimental validation on a test bench, in compliance with industrial electrical safety standards.
  • Built strong working knowledge of UPS architectures: rectifiers, inverters, bypass, 4th leg, booster/charger stages.

TECHNOPURE SAS · Belfort, France

Firmware for a connected air purifier: ambient data collection, remote control, component lifetime tracking.
